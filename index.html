Services Offered

Know exactly what your coins are worth.

Westmoreland Rare Coins is available to appraise your rare coins, hoards and accumulations for estate, insurance, or domestic-dispute purposes — or if you just want to know what your coins are worth in today's market.

$25 MIN · ~$100/HR SVC—01

Coin Appraisals

Professional appraisals for estates, insurance, disputes — or peace of mind. Prices range from a minimum of $25 on up, depending on the scope and detail involved; detailed appraisals currently bill at about $100/hr. Bookable as a 1-hour session.

We refund the appraisal fee if we purchase the coins within 30 days of the appraisal.
$100 · 30 MIN SVC—02

Third-Party Coin Review

We screen and submit only those coins that make economic sense to NGC or PCGS for third-party grading, or to CAC for certified acceptance — so you never waste money grading the wrong coins.

Any fees charged will be refunded if we purchase the coins 30 days or less from the service date.
SVC—03

Want List Fulfillment
